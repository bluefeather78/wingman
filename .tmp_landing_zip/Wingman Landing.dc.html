Why we built Wingman

It started with a spreadsheet — and a nagging fear of missing something.

When my son was in high school, I started looking for summer programs that would help him explore his interests and make the most of the few summers he had before college. What I quickly discovered was that finding the right opportunities was surprisingly difficult.

Programs often opened applications months before summer. Websites weren't always updated until close to the deadline. And there was no easy way to know which opportunities were actually a good fit for him — his interests, goals, schedule, and budget.

So I built an Excel sheet. I tracked programs, checked websites, watched for applications to open, and tried to keep up with deadlines. And no matter how carefully I kept track, there was always that nagging question: What if I missed something?

Wingman started as something I built for my own sons. I'm opening it up to other families because I believe this process shouldn't require endless Google searches, spreadsheets, bookmarks, and calendar reminders.

I built Wingman because high school is too short to spend it wondering what you might have missed.

Why We Built Wingman

Wingman started with a spreadsheet.

When my son was in high school, I began looking for summer programs that could help him explore his interests, learn something new, and make the most of the few summers he had before college. What I quickly discovered was that finding those opportunities was far harder than it should have been.

Many programs opened applications in the fall—months before summer was even on my radar. Websites often wouldn't be updated until surprisingly close to the deadline. So I created an Excel sheet and started checking program websites, one by one, trying to keep track of when applications would open and when they would close.

And I was constantly worried I would miss something.

That experience made me realize something bigger: high school students have a remarkably short window to explore what they care about. Four years. Three summers. A handful of opportunities to try something new, go deeper into an interest, meet people who share their passions, and discover what they might want to pursue next.

Yet finding those opportunities is still largely a matter of searching the internet, keeping spreadsheets, setting reminders, and hoping you haven't overlooked something.

And there is so much out there. The challenge isn't simply finding an opportunity. It's finding the right one—one that matches a student's interests, fits their schedule and budget, has a good reputation, and is actually worth pursuing. Then, somehow, remembering to apply before the deadline.

I built Wingman because I wanted something that would do that work for my family.

It started as a tool for my own sons. But as I built it, I kept thinking about how many other students and families were probably facing the same frustration.

Wingman is our attempt to make that process a little easier.

We want to help students discover opportunities they might never have found on their own, understand which ones are actually worth their time, and—most importantly—find opportunities without missing the deadline.

Because the goal shouldn't be spending your summer hunting for opportunities.

It should be spending it chasing what you're excited about.

That's why we built Wingman.

Why We Built Wingman

Wingman started with a spreadsheet, and a nagging fear of missing something. When my son was in high school, I started looking for summer programs that would help him explore his interests. What I quickly discovered was that finding the right opportunities was surprisingly difficult.

What if I missed something?

Wingman started as something I built for my own sons. I'm opening it up to other families because I believe this process shouldn't require endless Google searches, spreadsheets, bookmarks, and calendar reminders.

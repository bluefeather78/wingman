# Wingman Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Shama Bildikar**, the operator of Wingman ("Wingman," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, processes, and protects information in connection with the Wingman beta.

Wingman is designed to help high-school students discover and organize extracurricular activities, competitions, internships, programs, and other opportunities.

By using Wingman, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that users voluntarily provide to Wingman.

Depending on how you use the Service, this may include:

### Account information

- name;
- email address;
- account credentials or authentication information; and
- information necessary to establish and maintain your account.

### Student profile information

Users may choose to provide information such as:

- interests;
- activities;
- educational information;
- goals;
- location information;
- extracurricular experiences;
- preferences;
- college-preparation information; and
- other information they choose to include in their profile.

You control what information you provide to your profile.

### Resume and profile uploads

Wingman allows users to provide resume or profile information by:

- uploading a PDF or text file; or
- pasting profile information into Wingman.

Uploaded files are processed to extract or use relevant information for Wingman features. **Wingman does not retain the uploaded file itself after processing.**

Information extracted from or provided through an upload may become part of your Wingman profile and may be retained according to this Privacy Policy.

### Automatically collected information

We may automatically collect certain technical and usage information when you use Wingman, including information such as:

- browser and device information;
- IP address;
- approximate location derived from technical information;
- pages or features accessed;
- dates and times of access;
- basic usage information; and
- information collected through cookies or similar technologies.

We do not intentionally collect precise geolocation information.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to operate and improve Wingman.

For example, we may use information to:

- create and maintain user accounts;
- build and update user profiles;
- identify opportunities that may be relevant to users;
- rank opportunities based on information users provide;
- generate activity recommendations;
- organize saved opportunities;
- generate deadlines and tasks;
- provide and improve Wingman features;
- maintain security;
- detect misuse;
- understand how users interact with Wingman;
- troubleshoot technical problems;
- communicate with users about the Service; and
- develop and improve Wingman.

## 3. AI Processing

Wingman uses artificial intelligence to provide certain features.

Information contained in a user's profile may be sent to third-party AI service providers, including **Anthropic and Google**, to process information and provide features such as opportunity ranking, profile generation, recommendations, and other functionality.

We do not represent that third-party AI providers will retain or process information exactly as Wingman does. Their own policies and contractual arrangements may apply to their processing.

We do **not** intentionally use user information to train our own AI models.

We also do not represent that third-party providers will never use information for model training or other purposes outside Wingman's direct control. We will seek to use appropriate provider configurations and agreements consistent with our intended privacy practices.

## 4. Opportunity Recommendations

Wingman may use information in a user's profile to identify and rank opportunities.

Before a user's profile contains sufficient information for personalized ranking, Wingman may allow the user to browse available opportunities without personalized AI ranking.

Once sufficient profile information is available, Wingman may use that information to determine which opportunities may be more relevant to the user.

The recommendations are not guarantees and should not be interpreted as professional admissions or educational advice.

## 5. Information We Do Not Collect Intentionally

Wingman does not intentionally collect precise GPS location information.

Wingman also does not currently provide public profiles, public posts, social feeds, user-to-user messaging, or other features designed to make student profile information publicly available.

Your Wingman profile and activity information are intended to remain private.

## 6A. Mailing-List Signup

If you use Wingman's optional mailing-list signup feature, we send your first and last name and the email address you confirm at the time to the organization's mailing-list provider, so that they can add you to their list.

This happens only for an opportunity you individually select and confirm. We do not send your details to any organization otherwise.

We keep a record of each signup you make: which opportunity, which email address you used, when it was submitted, and what the organization's provider answered. You can see this record in your Quest Log. We keep it so you can find what was sent on your behalf, and so we can tell whether a signup went through.

We cannot see whether you completed the organization's confirmation step, and we do not receive the organization's emails to you. Once your details reach the organization, their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policy, not this one.
